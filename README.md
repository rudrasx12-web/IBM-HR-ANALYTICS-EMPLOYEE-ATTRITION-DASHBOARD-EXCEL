# 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is one of the most critical challenges faced by modern organizations. High turnover leads to increased hiring costs, reduced productivity, knowledge loss, and lower employee engagement.

This project transforms raw HR data into meaningful business intelligence using Microsoft Excel. It demonstrates the complete analytics workflow—from data cleaning and business calculations to executive dashboards and strategic recommendations.

The project was designed to simulate how Business Analysts and HR Analysts build reporting solutions that support data-driven decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

The HR department wants to answer the following business questions:

- Which department has the highest employee attrition?
- Which job roles experience the greatest turnover?
- Does overtime increase employee attrition?
- Which employees are at the highest retention risk?
- How does compensation differ across departments?
- What business actions can reduce future attrition?

# 💡 Key Business Insights

### 📌 Department Analysis

Sales recorded the highest employee attrition, indicating a need for targeted retention initiatives.

### 📌 Job Role Analysis

Several job roles experience significantly higher turnover than others, highlighting role-specific retention risks.

### 📌 Overtime Analysis

Employees working overtime demonstrate considerably higher attrition compared to employees with standard workloads.

### 📌 Compensation Analysis

Monthly income varies across departments and employee groups, creating opportunities for compensation optimization.

### 📌 Employee Tenure Analysis

Most employee exits occur during the early years of employment, emphasizing the importance of onboarding and employee engagement.

# 📌 Strategic Recommendations

Based on analytical findings, the following recommendations are proposed:

- Reduce excessive overtime through workforce planning.
- Strengthen onboarding and mentoring programs.
- Improve employee recognition initiatives.
- Develop department-specific retention strategies.
- Increase employee engagement through regular feedback.
- Support executive decision-making using KPI-driven dashboards.
